## 📚 Publication

### 🩸 Pre-Analytical Quality Validation for Malaria Diagnosis

**"Pre-Analytical Quality Validation for Malaria Diagnosis: A Multi-Stage Deep Learning and Computer Vision Framework"**

📍 18th International Conference on Electronics, Computers and Artificial Intelligence (ECAI 2026)  
📍 Bucharest, Romania  
📅 2026  
🏛️ IEEE

🔗 DOI: 10.1109/ECAI69016.2026.11613683

The research presents a multi-stage AI framework addressing the pre-analytical quality validation of Giemsa-stained blood smears for malaria diagnosis.

### 🔬 Research Components

🧪 **Staining Quality Analytics**
- Multi-task CNN
- Giemsa staining quality grading
- Optimal staining duration prediction
- Grad-CAM explainability

🩸 **Blood Smear Quality Assessment**
- Computer vision pipeline
- Contrast enhancement
- Pixel-to-mm calibration
- Region segmentation
- Smear morphology assessment

🔬 **Parasite Detection**
- EfficientNet
- YOLOv8
- Parasite localization
- Species identification
- Severity estimation

📖 A journal manuscript extending this research is currently in preparation.
